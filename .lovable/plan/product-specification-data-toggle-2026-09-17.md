# Product specification data toggle

Add the catalog’s Dimensions / Electrical Properties control to every individual product specification page.

## Changes
- Add a two-option data toggle to the product specifications section, defaulting to Dimensions.
- Dimensions view will show the selected product’s length, width, height, unit weight, industry reference, and standard terminal.
- Electrical Properties view will show all published capacity rates from 1 through 120 hours under the existing nominal-capacity heading.
- Keep the existing product imagery, overview, description, related products, and navigation unchanged.
- Use the catalog table’s borders, header hierarchy, monospace numeric values, alignment, responsive horizontal scrolling, and theme colors for consistency.

## Validation
- Check both toggle states on a product page in light and dark themes.
- Confirm the table remains readable on desktop and small screens without changing surrounding layout.
